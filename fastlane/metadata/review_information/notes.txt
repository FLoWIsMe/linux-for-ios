Tern is a local Unix terminal. All programs it runs (ssh, vim, git, curl, grep and others) are compiled into the app bundle as iOS frameworks; nothing is downloaded or interpreted at runtime beyond the user's own shell scripts and the commands they type, the same model as a-Shell (App Store id 1473805438). No account is needed. To try it: type "help", then "ls", "echo hello", "vim test.txt" (press i to type, Esc then :wq to save) and "ssh" to see usage. The app does not collect data. Source code: https://github.com/FLoWIsMe/linux-for-ios
