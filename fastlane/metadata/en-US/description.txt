Tern is a real Unix terminal for iPhone and iPad. It runs genuine command line programs on your device: ssh, scp, sftp, git (through libgit2), vim, curl, tar, grep, sed, awk, find, jq, lua, make and about a hundred more. Nothing is emulated and nothing leaves your phone unless you connect somewhere. It works offline and starts instantly.

A SHELL THAT FEELS LIKE HOME
Tern's shell understands the POSIX syntax you already know plus the bash features people actually use: pipelines, redirection, if, for, while, case, functions, $(...), $((...)), globbing, heredocs, [[ ]] and brace expansion. Scripts run with a real dash.

MADE FOR A PHONE KEYBOARD
A bar above the keyboard adds esc, tab, ctrl, alt, arrows and the symbols a shell needs. Modifiers latch, arrows repeat when held, and hardware keyboards get every shortcut: Cmd-T for a new tab, Cmd-K to clear, Cmd-1 to 9 to switch tabs.

EDITING THAT HELPS
Tab completion with candidate lists. Ctrl-R history search. Grey inline suggestions from your history, accepted with the right arrow. Syntax highlighting as you type. The git branch in your prompt.

SSH WITHOUT FRICTION
Generate a key with one tap, copy the public half, add hosts to ~/.ssh/config from a visual list and connect in one tap. Password logins work too.

YOUR FILES, IN FILES
Home is the app's Documents folder. It appears in the Files app, so you can drop files in, edit them with vim, and sync repositories with git.

LIGHT AND DARK, PROPERLY
Eleven terminal palettes including Solarized, Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, One Dark and Catppuccin. Three fonts. A chrome that follows the system or your choice.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking, no network use except the commands you run. Tern is free and open source under the MIT license.
